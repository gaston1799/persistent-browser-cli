# PBC Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PBC (Persistent Browser CLI) is a local command-line tool for controlling a
Chrome browser that runs on the user's computer.

## Data handled by PBC

PBC may process page text, page structure, URLs, screenshots, downloads,
uploads, and browser state when the user or an authorized agent runs a command.
This processing occurs locally on the user's computer. PBC does not operate a
developer-controlled service that receives this browser data, and PBC does not
include analytics, advertising, or telemetry collection.

PBC stores its browser profile and local working data on the user's computer.
That profile may contain login sessions and other sensitive browser data. Users
are responsible for protecting access to their computer and profile directory.

## Third-party services

When PBC visits or interacts with a website, that website receives and handles
data under its own privacy policy. Installing PBC, Node.js dependencies, Chrome,
or updates may also contact the applicable distribution services, including
GitHub, npm, Google, or Microsoft, under their respective policies.

## Data sharing and retention

The PBC developer does not sell personal information and does not receive or
retain browser data through PBC. Files, logs, screenshots, downloads, and
browser-profile data remain subject to the user's local storage and deletion
choices.

## Contact

Privacy questions may be submitted through the project's public issue tracker:
